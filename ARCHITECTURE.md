# Engineering contracts

## Dependency direction

`game-data → ConfigStore → Simulation → RenderState → Three.js renderer`.

Dev controls and snapshot/checkpoint adapters command Simulation through explicit APIs. Simulation imports neither Three.js nor DOM/storage APIs. Plain-data state is authoritative; renderer state is disposable. No backend, ECS framework or generic plugin system is required.

## Deterministic Defense simulation

The game uses fixed steps, authored five-lane admissions and explicit progression plans. Reproducibility depends on config, level, seed and inputs. Gameplay never uses Math.random(). Enemy composition/placement cursors, Giant and Supply opportunities remain independent of dedicated naval RNG. Presentation schedulers do not consume gameplay RNG.

Enemy waves/Carnival/Survival own enemy admissions; NavalPresence owns ship lifecycle; NavalBarrage owns attacks and clocks; levelEncounters supplies small authored component actions. Artillery owns authoritative flight, impact and casualties. Naval visibility cannot suppress Survival. New naval salvoPolicy opts into three successful singles followed by staggered pairs. The existing launchedSalvos counter, a separate saved gap RNG and one pending plain-data shot preserve locked distinct targets and per-launch warnings. Cooldown waits for the queued shot and final authoritative impact; whole-salvo admission and delayed capacity checks prevent partial admission or catch-up bursts. Tank introduction disables barrage and discards its queue while airborne shells resolve. Missing saved policy retains the historical simultaneous scheduler and exact RNG draws. Profile changes affect future admissions only. Blocked opportunities cannot build backlogs.

Optional authored `defenseTank` owns the Lv9 encounter, a single HP/phase/rectangle, entry/advance, flank admissions and post-impact attack clocks. It suppresses other enemy admission and new naval fire while leaving old shells to resolve. Its own saved RNG supplies distinct attack lanes. The accepted newly authored `salvoPolicy.mode: randomStaggered` adds independent saved cooldown/gap RNG streams and at most two plain-data pending shots (locked lane/next launch time). Whole-volley capacity is checked before target selection; delayed launches recheck available capacity and shift remaining deadlines after a late step rather than firing catch-up bursts. The volley drains before a phase-at-resolution cooldown draw. Explicit cancellation discards queued shots. Historical embedded plans with missing policy/rhythm retain the original simultaneous/fixed scheduler without migration, and snapshots copy the bounded queue. Tank shells use ordinary Artillery; renderer resources remain disposable. `combatStats` counts player shots, direct kills and successful throws at authoritative boundaries. Tank clear freezes gameplay and excludes the forced enemy cleanup from direct kills; presentation time owns the bounded explosion finale and clear overlay.

New Stage 1 now uses `manualPlayerCall` without HP-table requirements: after the Commander notice, only player input may call the one-use, potentially lethal raid. The same target helper places subsequent bombs beyond the Tank in Simulation and rendering. They never award player XP/stats or change encounter deadlines. Checkpoints save clocks, round progress and the warning-time Tank position and at most 12 impact anchors. Missing historical state stays absent; older embedded settings retain quota-based three rounds or six-round raids with three Tank hits. `lastStand` is never injected into restored settings; round counts and saved impact validation remain unchanged. The policy-gated manual button owns an ephemeral 800ms Level Up-style ring/eight-mote/icon-glow effect on presentationMs; Restore primes readiness silently without new saved fields. Historical plans never receive manual state. Transient events are consumed once and discarded on restore. Saved clocks reconstruct a shared-geometry three-bomber formation, three preallocated soft silhouette ground shadows and nine reusable heavy VFX slots. Each heavy smoke draw retains twenty instances with six compact charcoal inner/fourteen airy light-gray outer lobes. Their reconstruction uses saved impact age to apply the shared BattlefieldWind offset, with no accumulated presentation state. A short 0.28s white flash/0.65s fire phase yields to the original 5.6s smoke lifecycle; scorch anchors stay fixed. One 12-slot persistent GroundScorchMarks batch reconstructs from saved heavy impact anchors, merges duplicate coordinates, draws beneath lane paint and clears on scene reset; grenade scorch behavior is unchanged. SupportFlyover shares the accepted affine Z(raidAge) path with the audio pass envelope. Nine detuned engine oscillators share a reusable 520Hz low-pass/duck bus beneath master volume; Commander activity ramps its gain live, independent of the six unchanged whistles. A single affine Z(raidAge) path at constant altitude drives both aircraft and their exact X/Z ground shadows, with constant scale/heading/opacity. This is renderer-only: shadows draw before lane paint with no shadow maps or new save state. Commander radio seeks its remaining recorded warning on restore; the per-attempt queue preserves playing recordings and cannot gate combat. Manual readiness/call use separate one-time Commander queue identities backed by bounded segments of the same approved support buffer. Source duration prevents the first segment leaking into the second; restored messages seek remaining offsets and missing audio keeps subtitles. No ambient war scheduler is restored.

ConfigStore validates authored JSON and supported runtime overrides atomically. Production public URLs are content-addressed and preserve /topwar/; unchanged portraits/audio retain URLs across commits. Accepted character, weapon and environment meshes are procedural. No retired GLB is downloaded.

Simulation.restoreState performs strict transactional validation. Released Beachhead Defense six-, eight- and ten-level plans preserve their saved progression, movement rules, concurrent/sequential Carnival/Destroyer policy, discrete/continuous naval cooldowns, active shells and Survival deadlines. Grenade capacity in historical config is only a teaching-reward alias; continuing inventory accumulates safely. Staged Supply creation copies recoverySeconds into each crate; eligibility uses its saved recoverAtSeconds. New authored timing is 0.35 seconds, while schema compatibility defaults and historical/already-spawned 0.70-second clocks remain unchanged. The 0.35-second timing is accepted on Pixel 8; no save migration or stage-VFX change accompanies it.

Fresh progression plans author `casualtySetback: true`; absent means historical casualty behavior. On the first surviving casualty, `progression.pressure` captures the highest earned `{level, xp}`. Combat level drops one and XP becomes zero per surviving casualty; later XP grants maintain the separate lexicographic high-water mark. Future wave profiles and encounter unlocks consume that peak; weapon power and HUD consume current progression. Restore validates peak bounds/order and its saved opt-in policy, clones it as plain data and permits already-latched encounters at reduced combat levels without relaxing their clocks, RNG or ownership checks. Enemy state and schedules are never rewritten. Last-member death cannot receive a recruiting reward.

Tank state and statistics are optional plain-data checkpoint fields. Historical saves without authored tank configuration keep their original encounters and wire state; no tank or retrospective statistics are injected into them. New checkpoints capture all tank/phase/flank/attack clocks and statistics alongside existing shells and RNG. Boss Heavy/Giant admissions optionally stamp their absolute approach speed on each enemy; unstamped enemies and older configurations retain their existing movement policy. The existing entrance-cleared flag latches only the one-time shockwave. Entrance crushing sweeps the centered authored Tank rectangle against existing enemy collision circles using relative Z motion, clipped to the elapsed entrance interval. It removes only contacted enemies before the existing projectile/contact pass, without awards, RNG draws or schedule changes. Removed victims need no extra save ledger; restore uses saved Tank/enemy poses and clears disposable events, preserving remaining contacts without replaying shockwave, alarm or radio cues. Older Tank states remain readable. The legacy `boss: null` wire field remains inert.

A small set of inert wire fields (empty Boss/gate/reward/pickup state, zero rocket clocks, Tier-1 roster and stream cursors) remains to avoid breaking released Defense snapshots. Optional historical config/tuning metadata is parsed but has no retired gameplay implementation. Nonempty old Boss/rocket/gate/finite-formation or non-Defense prototype saves are unsupported; recovery offers a safe fresh start rather than reinterpreting them.

SessionCheckpoint uses one localStorage slot per DEV/release origin, format and simulation contract version 1, bounded to 1,048,576 characters before parsing/writing. Smaller layouts, member clocks and embedded historical progression plans/caps stay unchanged; there is no viewport-dependent Simulation or new checkpoint state. No per-frame serialization. VictoryCelebration owns bounded reusable post-clear tracers and a temporary lane pose on the existing pause-aware presentation clock. ## Rendering and audio

CoastalCamera owns the single PerspectiveCamera projection for every DEV/production scene and Continue. It preserves the approved P4.2 B calculation: move 4 units backward from (0, 6.5, -10) along the original axis toward (0, 0, 12.5), retain that target, and multiply the tangent of the aspect-adjusted 48° reference half-FOV by d/(d+4), where d is the original camera-space depth of (0, .8, 0). Recompute on resize and update both projection/world matrices before screen projections. Camera state is renderer-only; no camera mode, checkpoint field or alternate canvas exists. Physical Boss gameplay acceptance remains pending.

RenderState contains current player/enemy/projectile/Grenade/naval/artillery/progression data, never GPU objects. Actual artillery launch events trigger one reusable two-mesh flame/core per Tank/Destroyer, deduplicated by launch time and parented to the visible mouth, including turret recoil and mirrored ship motion. Four bounded smoke slots per cannon use three instanced light lobes and three darker lobes, sampled at the physical world muzzle after pose updates. Both layers detach and drift along render-world -X (screen-right under Camera B), with distinct rise/expansion/fade rates. Each sequential launch acquires a slot; simultaneous shells share one. Shared geometry and reusable transform scratch avoid per-frame allocations; reset/restore/disposal clear all plumes. BattlefieldWind supplies size-independent render-world -X smoke drift (screen-right) to cannon and reused Grenade/artillery-impact smoke. ExplosionSmoke shares bounded instanced shape, light/charcoal palette, opacity weighting and wind across artillery impacts (12 lobes/1.8s), Grenades (16/1.9s) and heavy airstrikes (20/5.6s). Artillery smoke has a 3.6-unit visual radius floor independent of collision and suppresses inherited grenade box debris/sparks; Grenades retain three overlapping slots. Each slot still uses one smoke draw; transform scratch and shader uniforms are reused. Non-smoke debris/flash/shock clocks, simulation events, damage and save state remain independent. Saved airborne shells cannot trigger it; restore/reset clears transient feedback. Authoritative shell origins remain unchanged. Instanced projectile and crowd batches, bounded effect pools and idle presentation preparation preserve mobile performance. The golden arcade tracer uses the same two batches and authoritative anchor/orientation with a thin tapered mesh and bounded pixel/world dimensions authored in ArtDirection; no projectile simulation or save fields depend on its visual footprint. Heavy HP bars, accepted VFX intensity and rendering resolution are not reduced. Retry/reset clears transient feedback; disposal releases owned resources once. Restoring state initializes observations without replaying launch, acquisition, Level Up or Observer cues.

BridgeEnvironment retains its name but owns the current coastal battlefield, transports, architecture and distant aircraft. WarActivityScheduler schedules aircraft only. Background infernos, random artillery/flak visuals and audio are retired; no associated resources or frame updates remain. BattlefieldAir owns only pooled Giant foot dust. Gameplay artillery and shared combat audio buffers remain independent.

Artillery warningPresentation is an optional saved presentation selector: new Stage 1 selects personalEmergency, while saved overheadLanes or absence retains historical rendering. Personal danger reuses artilleryHits with actual player X/Z and authoritative impact deadlines; it never writes Simulation. One reusable authored DOM glyph/tint follows the remaining 0.8 seconds. Glyph and screen pulses have separate simulation-driven cadences; Pause freezes them and Resume/Restore reconstruct the remaining phase. No personal siren exists. Ground highlights remain independently derived from active target lanes.

GameAudio uses one browser-activated AudioContext and prepared procedural buffers. Voice readiness is independent of optional portrait success. Pause and lifecycle suspension preserve synchronization; explicit Resume handles browser autoplay restrictions.

Boss and Victory results recordings reuse StreamedMusic, each with one lazy streaming HTMLAudioElement connected once to the existing master bus. No PCM song buffer or new checkpoint field is allocated. Boss position derives from the saved Tank encounter age; phase changes do not restart it. Stage1 Complete starts only with a non-null results clock and loops against that clock, after explosions and Commander speech. The browser handles Xing delay/padding at the loop boundary. Pausing/background silences both media and procedural sources, and Retry/Dispose cancel pending playback/fade cleanup. Procedural Boss and Victory music are exclusively pending/error fallbacks, suppressed while their recordings play. Existing presentation clocks, music volume and radio ducking are retained.

## Browser lifecycle and tools

Hidden/pagehide/freeze/context loss suspend simulation and held input, save and release Screen Wake Lock. Returning visible remains paused. Wake Lock acquisition is race-safe, optional and secure-context-only; failures cannot block gameplay. The DEV QA sleep scenario suppresses reacquisition until exited. Historical release QA checkpoints still restore their Defense state; public builds ignore the old armed-test flag and permit ordinary Resume.

The closed-playtest build shares exactly four scene buttons and their minimal initializers with local Vite. PlaytestScenarios selects Naval entrance, Boss entrance, Victory and the canonical manual 3-MG Air Support initializer in TankPlaytest. Ordinary authored Stage 1 now uses the manual policy; historical saved automatic policies stay embedded in checkpoints. GameApp creates a hidden native support control for Defense, derives its visibility/requests/readiness communication from the active authoritative manual policy, and consumes guarded one-use input at a fixed tick in both builds. Pause/background clear requests; inert/focus/menu guards remain shared. Historical shared air-support Continue restores saved policy/state; its Retry uses the historical battle initializer and saved balance, while explicitly choosing a fresh scene returns to authored manual review. Temporary selector fixtures are retired and rejected via normal invalid-checkpoint recovery, without a permanent experimental compatibility subsystem. DEV keeps the dense Tank review, advanced tuning and nested sleep/checkpoint QA; ScreenWakeLock observes directly into QaPanel and disposal removes the listener. No duplicate Wake status wrapper or URL launcher remains. The checkpoint envelope still carries optional Victory-preview progress; normal saves and clocks are unchanged. Browser automation exposes private app references only by intercepting test responses. Artifacts are ignored. Deployment remains manual workflow_dispatch.
