# Current visual contract

The accepted direction is Rounded Toy Soldier / Handcrafted Military Arcade, with Command Blue UI and a sunlit coastal Greek battlefield. Color layers separate olive Grunts, gray-blue/khaki Heavies, rust-brown/old-iron Giants and deep navy-gray vehicles. Sand/copper trim and small amber accents retain their shared armory language. Rounded infantry and chamfered machinery distinguish roles; friendly blue art stays unchanged. Models use the existing procedural, merged/instanced pipeline.

- Camera: the tested P4.2 Camera B is approved as the default in DEV and production after Pixel 8 preference testing. Keep the four-unit dolly along the original view axis, original look-at target and near-player depth/FOV compensation (about 36.3° at 9:16), with responsive aspect handling. All scenes, including Boss, Victory and Air Support, share it. Physical Boss gameplay acceptance remains pending; retain the Boss quick-test entry.
- Blue Squad: broad helmet, rounded toy body, charcoal Rifle/MG and accepted recoil/casualty poses.
- Grunt: light olive uniform, simple narrow pot helmet and exposed toy-soldier face; readable gait/death transitions.
- Heavy: cute, stout human armored infantry with a gray-blue padded torso/thick round shoulders, convex khaki belly armor, large round khaki helmet and visible paired eyes/cheeks. Small olive pouches, wrist armor and broad stance distinguish it from Grunt. Visible navy-backed/red-fill HP bar, accepted hit/flash and lethal collapse.
- Giant: rust-brown armor, copper chest plating and old iron/blue-gray hardware with a small amber sensor slit. Retain the original offset rectangular head, left ammunition vault/shield arm, heavy legs and short right-hand breaching ram. No human face or oversized soldier costume. Preserve accepted 172 HP, weapon grip, weighted gait, reveal, HP bar, foot dust and grounded reactions.
- Ink Spear: exact permanent black pointed silhouette and narrow ivory core, two pooled batches, bounded perspective-aware scaling. No temporary Level Up color/glow transformation.
- Golden Grenade: authored gold model, acquisition transfer, distinct short launch confirmation, bounded luminous flight feedback and accepted explosion/scorch/blood aftermath.
- Destroyer: deep navy-gray long hull with a sand armor belt, layered armored bridge/amber observation slit, blocky radar and thick main gun; four merged material batches. Preserve offshore entrance/station/independent retreat, exact muzzle position, flash, shell flight and impact. Slightly uneven inverted triangle outlines and solid-red exclamations share a fixed bright-red material; interiors always stay transparent. All five locked lane markers sit overhead at world Y=2.05 with uncompressed authoritative lane X, the same rendering-only +1.2-unit Z offset and 75% silhouette/glyph width. Keep the full red exclamation and most of each outer triangle visible on portrait phones, retaining the existing Camera B outer-outline clipping (about 30% of width at peak pulse); physical Boss warning readability remains pending Pixel 8 acceptance. No edge fitting, individual lane correction or warning-specific camera/FOV changes; marker placement never changes shell.target or damage. Escalating scale pulse is the only urgency animation (2–4 beats/s in the last second, extra final-150ms emphasis); no progressive fill or ground circles for current Stage 1.
- Tank Boss: deep navy-gray armored wall across the middle three lanes, broad sloped front armor/cheeks, huge iron tracks, wide turret, ivory structural trim, thick dark main gun and recessed amber observation slits. Brief additive ivory/yellow flashes stay on hull/turret, with a stronger Grenade flash and actual HP-damage text; ship doors never flash. Ship doors and ramp introduce it in 2.5 seconds. Scars and stronger phase-3 exhaust/vibration convey pressure while preserving the base armor color. A bounded sequence of explosions ends in a large blast and a visible charred wreck with three small orange/gold fires and slowly rising dark smoke.
- Air support: three broad-winged, four-engine iron/navy-gray bombers arrive from below behind friendly lines, fly forward along lanes 0/2/4 and depart toward the sea. Retain huge fireballs, dark smoke, shockwaves and enemy tumbling. New raids show one real Tank hit; subsequent bombs visibly land beyond it.
- Coastal environment: ivory architecture, blue shutters/sea, warm sand, cloth/vegetation/foreground, clouds and atmospheric aircraft. BridgeEnvironment owns the active scene despite its historical name.
- 海防指揮官: retain the same young female officer, royal-blue cap/uniform and gold insignia. Command uses neutral, Alert uses alert; forthcoming victory.webp should show a sincere, proud smile and currently falls back to neutral without requesting missing art. Fixed upper communication slot for all messages, 110×110 breakout composition, Traditional Chinese subtitles and radio cues. Tank entrance uses a faint full-screen red alarm with retained border beneath HUD/subtitles; active Tank HP follows the turret with numbers inside its bar and no persistent titles. Preserve provenance in public/art/observer/PROVENANCE.md.
- Victory: one lightly darkened battlefield poster, large gold title, visible central wreck, direct left-side hero numbers and small secondary text; no information cards or panels. The complete transparent Commander/soldier illustration occupies roughly a quarter of the viewport, with results painted above it for readability. The same Commander rests her hand on the blue-helmet soldier’s shoulder; static coherent art is preferred to cutout limb animation. Gold Replay closes the short staggered reveal.
- HUD: accepted bottom lane controls, XP/loadout and compact Grenade count. Quiet 44px testing hatch shared by local Vite and closed-playtest Pages; subtle 14px FPS is local DEV-only. No new backing cards or overlap with Pause/subtitles/safe areas.

Use existing palette/material/VFX modules, pooled resources and bounded preparation. Physical-phone review is authoritative for readability; desktop projected pixels and SwiftShader are supporting diagnostics only. Historical art-showcase exports and unused GLBs are archived in Git history.
