# Accepted Beachhead Defense gameplay

This is the current closed-playtest contract. Balance values live in `public/game-data/game.json`; this document does not authorize further tuning.

## Camera

The tested P4.2 Camera B is approved for normal DEV and production play after Pixel 8 preference testing. Its weaker perspective improves distant-lane separation while preserving near-player composition. Every gameplay and quick-test scene, including Boss, Victory and Air Support, uses the same projection. Physical Boss gameplay acceptance remains pending; the existing Boss entry stays available. Camera projection changes neither authoritative coordinates nor saved gameplay state.

## Battlefield lane highlights

P4.3 is approved for implementation; visual acceptance is pending Pixel 8 playtesting. One subtle cyan/sea-blue ground corridor follows the player physical X during lane transitions and fades toward distant enemies. Active enemy artillery highlights its authoritative target lanes in translucent red immediately after launch, for both Destroyer and Tank Boss. A lane remains red until its final shell resolves or is cancelled; simultaneous lanes are supported. Red takes priority over cyan, retaining a small cyan position cue near the player without muddy color mixing. Existing overhead triangles and their pulse remain unchanged. There are no five permanent roads, emergency exclamation or new alarm. Highlights derive only from current render state, freeze with Pause and reconstruct on Continue/Restore; Retry clears old threats. The firing cue disappears when the squad is lost or the Tank is defeated, preserving Victory presentation.

## Controls and combat

Five stationary lanes; player Z=0. Hold left/right for adjacent lane steps, or use A/D/arrows. Touch uses the most recently pressed finger; releasing it restores the newest remaining finger. Same-direction fingers share one cadence without duplicate steps. Direction changes step immediately, then repeat after 180ms at 120ms intervals. In portrait touch mode, transparent hit areas reach the viewport’s lower corners and safe inset, extend 13px above the visible faces, and cover 20% of each XP end while preserving its center and the unchanged button art. Pause/end/retry, lost focus and page hiding clear held input. The squad fires automatically along its selected corridor. Projectiles, swept collision, damage, targeting and RNG are authoritative Simulation behavior. A direct enemy contact or artillery hit removes one living soldier; losing the last soldier is Game Over. Weapon-stage upgrades do not undo casualties.

## Ordinary pressure and Giant

The dangerous Lv3→4 transition is retained. At Lv4 XP≥35%, ordinary waves contain two Heavies across four pressure lanes; at Lv5 XP≥25%, three Heavies across three lanes. Thresholds do not change wave population or cadence. Giant introduction occurs on the tick reaching Lv4, at the distant spawn position, in a least-crowded interior lane: 172 HP, 0.68 authored speed, 120 XP, once per run. Ordinary combat determines its survival.

Grunts have 1 HP; Heavies have 15 HP. Existing approach rules, lane cursors and stamped Survival speed multipliers are preserved.

## Carnival, naval presence and Survival

Lv6 Evolution admits the one-time 60-enemy release (one Heavy), starts Carnival and introduces the Destroyer without clearing existing enemies. Carnival owns ordinary admissions for 3.2 seconds, retaining its +2-second Grunt opportunity (up to 36, maximum 80 active enemies for Carnival admissions) and accepted lane patterns. There is no parallel ordinary wave stream.

The Observer warning begins at +1.3 seconds. At MG Release +3.2 seconds, Carnival ends as the Destroyer finishes entering, and AHEAD Survival takes over. Its first mixed wave arrives at +4.2 seconds: 26 Grunts and four Heavies across three lanes at Lv6–7, followed by the existing six-second cadence. Higher levels use the profiles below. Barrage activation remains at +8.5 seconds, with a separate two-second initial firing delay; the first attack is at the first fixed tick at +10.5 seconds. The Destroyer remains offshore. Historical checkpoints retain their own Carnival and encounter schedules; the original five scripted launches are historical save behavior only.

Current Stage 1 salvos contain one or two shells, 50/50, in distinct randomly selected lanes. Only one unresolved salvo is allowed. After its final authoritative impact, draw one floating-point uniform quiet gap in [2,5) seconds from the dedicated naval RNG; launch at the first eligible fixed tick. No repeated draws, catch-up bursts or automatic difficulty mitigation. Historical configurations retain launch-based or discrete-gap semantics. Shell trajectory, collision, damage, impact effects and audio are unchanged.

Warnings use slightly uneven bright-red inverted triangle outlines and matching solid-red exclamations. Their interiors remain transparent for the entire flight, with no fill animation. Each marker is centered overhead at world Y=2.05 above its locked target lane, using authoritative target X without horizontal compression and a uniform rendering-only Z of target Z + 1.2 units; it does not follow player movement or point at the ground impact pixel. All five lanes use the same complete geometry, narrowed to 75% of its previous width while retaining height and pulse. The promoted Camera B retains natural outer-lane outline clipping (about 30% of width at peak pulse on the checked portrait sizes); the full red exclamation and most of the triangle remain visible. Physical Boss warning readability remains pending Pixel 8 acceptance. There is no edge fitting, per-lane adjustment or warning-specific camera/FOV change; the actual damage position remains the unmodified shell.target. Color and opacity stay fixed. The only urgency animation is the existing scale pulse, growing stronger and faster over the final second (2–4 beats/s), plus extra emphasis in the final 150ms. Each lane uses its earliest incoming impact until the last shell resolves. Pause freezes the pulse; restore reconstructs it from active shell deadlines. Current Stage 1 has no ground warning circles.

## Golden Grenades

The Lv3 teaching Supply appears after eight seconds and grants three Grenades; recurring Supplies grant one. Three is a teaching reward, never an inventory cap. Inventory is a nonnegative safe integer with checked addition up to Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER. The reward event reports only the actual award; numerical overflow cannot produce false acquisition feedback. Holding 20 or 1000 does not block recurring Supplies or allocate proportional resources.

A successful throw consumes one Grenade and creates one authoritative flight. One flight at a time; Q and the HUD use the same input path. Existing global threat selection, 0.65-second flight, 9-HP damage and radius 4 remain unchanged. A blast overlapping active Tank armor applies the same 9 HP once, with a stronger local armor flash and actual damage text beside Boss HP; nearer flank enemies may still be selected as the throw's target. Launch sound and gold HUD pulse occur only for an actual throw. Acquisition transfer, launch and explosion feedback remain distinct. Staged crate destruction retains its three stages and 0.7-second recovery windows.

## Commander battlefield support

The actual first living Giant admission awards one Alert radio notice, with 3.8-second Traditional Chinese subtitles and Radio Open/Close, without a substitute recording. Retry rearms it; restoring an already-seen Giant never replays it.

Each newly configured Boss battle has one last-stand heavy bomber raid. The Tank must be active in phase 3; either HP is at most 1000 (no flank requirement), or a living Heavy/Giant in either simulation lane 0/4 has ETA at most 9.5 seconds. ETA is `(enemy.z - actualDefenseLineZ) / enemyApproachSpeed`, including the enemy's stamped Boss flank speed. The threshold is the 7.5-second first-bomb delay plus a configured 2-second margin. The existing 5.5-second entry/overlapping naval-radio guard remains. Phase 2 cannot trigger the raid. No squad-health/performance condition, enemy freeze or encounter-schedule change is used.

The approved 6.766-second Commander recording has two subtitles, switching at +3.05s. Three four-engine bombers arrive from behind the player at small world Z, fly forward along lanes 0/2/4, then continue toward the sea at increasing Z and shrink with perspective. Fixed-step bombing sweeps near/middle/far bands at +7.5/+8.15/+8.8s. Side bands cover 0–14, 14–28 and 28–42 units ahead; the central bands divide the saved player-to-Tank distance into thirds. A short trailing overlap follows troop movement between impacts. Each impact kills its area's Grunts/Heavies and subtracts 85% of maximum HP from Giants, clamped to zero without healing. After reaching the Tank, one central hit at +9.45s deals `min(500, max(0, tank.hp - 150))`, with actual damage text and armor flash. Later central bombs at +10.1/+10.75s land 14/28 units beyond the moving Tank; if it was already at or below 150 HP, the first central bomb instead lands 7 units beyond it without damage. The raid cannot heal or finish the Boss: normal player attacks must land the final hit. Nine pooled heavy fire/shock/dust/black-smoke slots and bounded tumbling accompany distinct engine/whistle/heavy-impact audio. Aircraft lead the visible 0.6-second bomb drops and leave toward the sea over ten seconds; smoke lasts 5.6 seconds. No XP, player kills, shot counts or Grenades are awarded/consumed. Squad, Supply, live artillery and original spawn/attack schedules remain unchanged.

Warning/bomb clocks, completed rounds, the Tank position at warning and at most 12 plain-data impact anchors are checkpoint data. Pause freezes speech, countdown, flight and effects; Restore seeks remaining speech and rebuilds planes/smoke without replaying completed damage or radio-open cues. Defeat/Game Over cancels pending bombs. The six-round state/validation is unchanged. Released heavy-raid checkpoints without `lastStand` retain their embedded phase-2/3, bilateral 25-unit trigger and three 500-HP Tank hits, including waiting/completed rounds. Earlier support checkpoints retain their embedded 14-unit, 1.25-second warning, three-round 80% quota behavior without Tank damage; absent historical support stays absent. Radio priority remains Victory > Tank entry > air support > Destroyer > Giant > mission, preserving playing official MP3s. Asset failure cannot delay authoritative damage.
