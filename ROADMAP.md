# TopWar — Current Roadmap

## Current playable

- Catharsis prototype experiment: five configurable attack corridors, larger 1-HP Grunts, sparse slower 15-HP amber Heavies, and deterministic one/two-lane pressure held across several groups.
- Discrete left/right taps or key presses select a defended lane; lane-tagged Rifle shots reliably hit dense seeded crowds in that corridor. Keyboard holding uses a controlled repeat cadence. Rifle starts at 3 Hz. Fifty-member waves enter near the shoreline within bounded five-unit crowd depth; TUNE tests 10–100. Defenders stand on a beach with natural obstacle openings.
- Pause, TUNE, Game Over and Retry remain operational. Plain-data snapshots retain lane selection, projectile/enemy lane identity, experiment balance and stream cursors.
- Bosses, rewards, normal tier escalation and ENEMY LV are temporarily disabled. Existing Merge, tier power and the disabled systems remain in code for later experiments. No XP or weapon progression is added.
- Instanced toy soldiers, contact/death feedback, audio, environment and procedural music remain.

## NOW

**Playtest Phase 2.7 Overwhelm Threshold on portrait phones.** Compare 10/50/100-member crowds compressed onto the beach at 3 Hz, with fixed 15-hit Heavies and unchanged slow approach. Find when lane pressure makes future support desirable. Measure performance without treating it as the design limiter yet; no progression is added. This is a focused prototype, not the full new design.
