# Release candidate readability screenshots

These 720 × 1280 images were captured from the actual `GameRenderer` with loaded runtime GLBs in Vite dev. A temporary local fixture supplied fixed `GameRenderState` snapshots so center, edge, enemy tiers, Boss, and reward damage stages are directly comparable. The fixture was removed after capture. The live gameplay route was also checked separately.

- `center.png`: five player soldiers and five complete Tier-1 enemy rows, seven per row.
- `edge.png`: squad at the authored rightmost track position; partial outer-member crop is accepted.
- `intact.png`: Tier-3 intact reward with blue-family soldier helmet.
- `near.png`: same reward at 9/10 hits, with orange warning lid, cracks, and nearly empty durability bar.
- `tiers.png`: Tier-1, Tier-2, and Tier-3 enemy rows.
- `boss.png`: Tier-2 Boss at its existing gameplay visual scale.
